How to play

  • Pick a building at the bottom and put it on the island. Free villagers build it for you.
  • Tap a workplace and use plus and minus to choose how many villagers work there.
  • Villagers eat every day. Keep foragers, fishers or a farm with a bakery going.
  • Cottages bring new settlers. Wells, lanterns and flowers keep them happy.
  • Three goals run side by side: Grow, Prosper and Discover. Tap the goal card for details, requests, badges and festivals.
  • Tap any resource at the top for the village ledger: what came in and what was used, per day.
  • Join workplaces to a Storehouse with a path: their goods then travel by handcart and a quarter more arrives.
  • Tap a cottage to see its neighbourhood: warmth, company, rest and a lovely street all make its residents happier.
  • Now and then the village asks you to decide something. Take your time: the game waits.
  • Build an Academy for research: it unlocks new buildings and upgrades. Old sites on the island can be explored.
  • The seasons turn every four days. Stock up before winter, when berries and fruit are scarce.
  • Everyone sleeps at night. The night passes faster by itself.

Controls

Mouse: drag to move, scroll to zoom, right button to rotate. Keys: WASD moves, Q and E rotate, R turns a building, space pauses.

Touch: drag with one finger, pinch to zoom, twist two fingers to rotate. Tap a spot first, then tap Place.
